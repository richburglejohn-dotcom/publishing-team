# Author's Note — On Kaldi's

*Draft for inclusion in The Scarlet Foundation (and/or the Author's Note section of the completed Kaldi's Series). Place at the front or back of the manuscript — front matter is more common for context readers need going in; back matter works if you want the story to land first.*

---

**AUTHOR'S NOTE**

Kaldi's Coffee House was a real place — 941 Decatur Street, in the heart of the French Quarter, open from 1990 until 2000. For those of us who spent time there, it wasn't just a coffee shop. It was a living room for the Quarter's artists, musicians, students, and wanderers, and for a lot of us, it was where we became who we were going to be.

Baron Vieux's time behind that counter is fiction. The Baron Vieux you'll meet in these pages is a character, and the events of his life at Kaldi's are imagined. But the place itself — the espresso machine, the Venetian Crème, the forty-foot ceilings, the sense that you'd found somewhere you belonged — that part is true, and it's written with nothing but love for what Kaldi's was and what it meant to the people who walked through its doors.

This book is a tribute to that place and to everyone who remembers it the way I do. If you were there, I hope you feel it in these pages.

— Lejohn Richburg III

## 2. Author's Note — On The Apothecary

*Use once written consent is received. Pairs with the Kaldi's note above — can run as one combined Author's Note section, or as two short separate notes.*

**AUTHOR'S NOTE**

The Apothecary is a real bar in New Orleans, and much of what happens within these pages is drawn from my own year and a half working there. Its owner, Marita Crandle — an author in her own right — was kind enough to welcome this story once she learned it existed, and her support means more to me than she probably knows.

The characters and events depicted are fictionalized, and any resemblance to real staff, regulars, or nights on that floor is shaped by memory and imagination working together. But the spirit of the place — and the fact that working there is part of why I started writing at all — is entirely true.

— Lejohn Richburg III

# Bayouboujee — Author's Note (On the Recipes)

> **RETIRED 2026-10-18 — do not publish or adapt.** Superseded by the recipe-attribution line in `references/series-bible.md` (Bayouboujee section). Kept for history only.

*Draft — pending review with Clytemnestra. She's the source, so her input on wording matters more than usual here.*

---

**AUTHOR'S NOTE**

Bayouboujee was a real restaurant before it was a fictional one, and the food in these pages exists because of my wife, Clytemnestra Z. Scott — known to the stage as Rainey Vexen. I was the front of the house: oysters, meeting the room, keeping the floor moving. She was the backbone. Every recipe in this series that carries any real precision — the exact cup, the exact dash, the specific cut — is hers.

Clytemnestra is a songwriter first. She's been writing since she was five years old, in the back seat of her parents' station wagon, and she's written hundreds of songs since. What I didn't understand until I watched her run a kitchen is that she writes recipes the same way she writes a song — every measurement documented like a lyric that has to land exactly right, nothing left to guess at, nothing approximate. Baron Vieux's obsession with precision, his refusal to write anything down until it's earned, all of that came from watching her work.

This book carries her fingerprints more than mine, even where her name doesn't appear on the page. I wanted that written down somewhere true, not just something I said once and let it go.

— Lejohn Richburg III

# Kay Dorgenois, RN — Written Consent Request (Keilia Love)

*Purpose: Kay is inspired by Keilia. Get her OK in writing before Kay appears in anything published (book, blog, social, podcast, art). Same practice as the Marita request above. Draft 2026-10-18. Lejohn sends it in his own words; edit freely.*

Subject: Something I've been writing, and I want your blessing

Keilia,

There's something I've been working on that I want to tell you about before anyone else sees it.

The next Culinary Crescendo book brings a new woman into Baron's life: Kay Dorgenois, a night-shift nurse from New Orleans who wants to become a nurse practitioner. She's inspired by you. Your strength, the way you care for people, and where you're headed.

I want to be clear about what that means and what it doesn't:

1. **Kay is a character, not you.** Her name, her hospital, and everything that happens to her on the page are made up. I won't use your real name, your workplace, your family, or anything private about your life.
2. **The spirit is yours.** The heart of her, the part that makes her worth writing, comes from you, and I'd like to be able to say that.
3. **Her image is AI-generated.** The picture I'm using for Kay isn't a photo of you, and I won't use real photos of you without asking.

Would you be comfortable confirming in writing (a reply to this message is enough) that you're okay with:

1. A character inspired by you appearing in the Culinary Crescendo series (the books and the posts, videos, and podcast that go with them), and
2. Me mentioning, in an author's note or interview, that she was inspired by someone real, with or without your name, whichever you prefer?

If there's anything you'd want kept out, handled differently, or never put on a page, tell me and it stays out. And if you'd rather not be part of it at all, that's okay too. I'd rather know now.

— Lejohn
