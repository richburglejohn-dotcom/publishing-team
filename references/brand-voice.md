## VOICE RULES
- **Funny ↔ Serious:** mostly serious/literary, occasional wit in the character banter (Baron/Calliope/Rampart dialogue is dry and playful; narration and captions stay controlled)
- **Bold ↔ Humble:** bold about the story and the craft behind it; the one first-person post so far (`docs/blog.html`, "The Real Baron Vieux") is notably humble and unguarded — that register exists but is rare, reserved for personal/tribute content
- **Casual ↔ Polished:** polished, literary — full sentences, deliberate rhythm, not text-to-a-friend casual. Short punchy fragments are used for effect ("Six people. One bottle."), not as a constant style
- **Short ↔ Storyteller:** storyteller by default — most posts run a hook, a beat, a turn, then the CTA. TikTok scripts are the most compressed format; Facebook/blog posts run longest
- **NEVER (per this session's actual corrections, not guesses):** real-world trademarked business names in any prop/copy (the Galatoire's key-tag issue); invented specific event dates/venues stated as fact (the "Blue Bayou, Oct 18" flyer, the "1 day countdown, doors 7pm" card); silently "fixing" a continuity or character-likeness error instead of flagging it; hashtag walls beyond the established bank

# PLATFORM STYLES

## TIKTOK
- 30-40 sec scripts, hook in first 2-5 seconds, usually text-on-screen driven since Google Flow doesn't render spoken dialogue reliably outside single lines
- Structure that's worked repeatedly: hook line → 2-3 beats building tension/context → turn/reveal → title card → CTA
- Silent text-card builds are an accepted fallback format when Flow footage or recorded VO isn't ready in time (`outputs/tiktok-book1-partnobody-v1.mp4`, `outputs/tiktok-ghost-spotlight-v1.mp4`)
- Posted manually — no TikTok API upload

## INSTAGRAM
- Quote cards and Reels both in use; hashtag bank capped at the 20-tag list in `outputs/social-content-book1-deepdive-v1.md`, not unlimited
- Carousel format used for ensemble/crew content (`outputs/apothecary-crew-carousel-v1/`)

## FACEBOOK
- Longer-form than IG/X, conversational but still literary — this is where the personal/author-voice posts have lived (Mike tribute, blog companion pieces)
- Off-schedule personal content is explicitly allowed here when it's genuine, not manufactured

## X / TWITTER
- Short, quote-driven, one strong line or fragment per tweet, sometimes threaded (see `outputs/scarlet-foundation-launch-announcement-v1.md` for a working thread example)

## FACEBOOK ADS / META ADS MANAGER
