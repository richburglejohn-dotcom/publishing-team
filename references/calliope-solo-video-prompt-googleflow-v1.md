## CHARACTER REFERENCE (reuse across all scenes)
**Calliope** *(pronounced "cal-LEE-ope")* — Black woman, dark skin, sharp-eyed and steady, unhurried, freckles across nose/cheeks, small nose ring and hoop earrings. Extensive tattoo work on both forearms (florals, rose, snake/dagger, skull, fine linework). Dark button-up shirt (olive-green or black) with rolled sleeves, sometimes a brown waist apron. Match face/hair/wardrobe/tattoos to the reference art in every clip.

**Setting — her bar** — Dim, warm apothecary-style tincture bar, dark wood counter, exposed bulb pendant lights, glass jars of dried botanicals, "Scarlet Elixir" bottles on the back shelf, hand-painted "Apothecary" signage.

**Visual:** Calliope leans on the bar with arms loosely crossed, watching the room with a sharp, unreadable half-smile — patrons blurred and anonymous in the background, no named characters. Three-quarter shot.
**Mood/Lighting:** Warm amber bar light, haze in the air.
**Audio:** Distant murmur of the room, no dialogue.

### SCENE 3 — Last Call
**Visual:** Calliope pours a small drink for herself at the end of the night and studies it quietly before setting it down, unhurried and private. Close-up.
**Mood/Lighting:** Most lights dimmed except her lamp, intimate and low.
**Audio:** Quiet clink of the glass, no dialogue.

### SCENE 4 — Locking Up
**Visual:** Calliope steps outside her bar alone, turns to lock the door behind her, streetlamp glow against the night. Wide shot.
**Mood/Lighting:** Warm streetlamp glow against cool night blue.
**Audio:** Key turning in the lock, faint night street ambiance.
