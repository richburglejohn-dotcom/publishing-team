How It Works

The Formulary Method — six phases, each with a checklist, a template, and a Loom walkthrough.

  1. Positioning Lock

    Audience, promise, table of contents, competing titles.

  2. Research Pack

    Sources, stories, facts to verify.

  3. Drafting

    Voice-trained prompts for intro, core chapters, and close.

  4. Human Refinement

    Our edit pass for voice, accuracy, and originality.

  5. Production

    Cover brief, interior design, formatting.

  6. Distribution

    KDP setup, description, keywords, launch assets.

Questions

Is this ghostwritten by AI?

No. You provide expertise, stories, and review. AI assists drafting, we do human refinement for voice and accuracy. You own the work.

Will Amazon accept it?

Yes, when done right with human originality, disclosure where required, and your expertise. We teach responsible publishing.

What if I have no manuscript?

Perfect. That's what the system is for. Start with expertise, not pages.
