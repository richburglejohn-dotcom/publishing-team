Late at night, after the rush, Olivia's alone at the end of the bar. Baron's in the kitchen, working up the nerve to bring something out.

He calls it the unthinkable. He's put roe on a cake.

He carries it across the room like he's making an entrance — because he is.

He sets it down in front of her, both hands up, theatrical as ever. "You will notice that I have done the unthinkable. I have made the roe... wait."

Behind him, Rampart doesn't even look up. "Destiny. Last week it was the bisque."

Olivia takes the bite. Cake, frosting, roe, all at once. She sets the fork down slow.

"Okay," she says. "That's a whole bit."

Baron walks back to the kitchen lighter than he left it, humming something under his breath. He'd never admit it, but that's the whole reason he made it.
