## The alcohol flag (P4, P5) — held for Lejohn, not silently filed
Per `references/brand-voice.md`, the established rule is **no visible alcohol in Meta Ads creative** (the organic-with-drink variants are filed separately and marked not-for-ads). These two are organic promo, where bar/drink imagery has precedent (Calliope the bartender; the Apothecary is a bar). But two things make it worth surfacing:
1. **Baron's arc is sobriety/recovery** — a recurring, load-bearing series theme. Prominent beer/wine in promo art can read against that.
2. **Ad-ineligible as-is** — if either post is ever boosted via Meta Ads, the alcohol disqualifies this creative.

**Options:** (a) use as-is for organic only; (b) regenerate clean (no-alcohol) versions so they're ad-eligible and fully on-theme. Claude recommends (b) for P5 at least, given the sobriety thread. Lejohn's call — not changed without it.
