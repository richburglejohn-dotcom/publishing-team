### CHARACTER REFERENCE (reuse across every scene)

**Baron Vieux** — Lean, deliberate build, dark-skinned, sharp-featured, square-framed black glasses, short hair. Charcoal-gray chef's coat or plain dark apron, worn late, sleeves pushed up. Calm exterior with visible fatigue underneath — a man running on discipline more than rest. **Do not render embroidered coat text** ("Baron Vieux" / "Bayouboujee") — repeatedly comes out garbled in Flow; leave fabric plain.

**Calliope** — Black woman, confident and steady behind her own bar, natural short coily hair, sharp-eyed and unhurried, the kind of presence that watches people reveal what their faces work to conceal. Simple black button-up shirt, sleeves rolled, black apron. Match `references/calliope-portrait-v1.jpg` and her established look from `references/roe-velvet-video-prompt-googleflow-v1.md` — same woman, same bar.

**Setting — Calliope's bar** — A separate, smaller neighboring bar Baron walks to after service. Dim, warm amber light. Dark wood bar counter. Bottles of Sazerac Rye visible on the back shelf. Soft string lights and a few blurred regulars or an arched doorway/window out of focus in the background. Quiet at this hour — the post-rush lull before last call. Same room as the Roe Velvet scenes — keep it visually consistent.

**Setting — the Apothecary kitchen** — French Quarter restaurant kitchen, exposed brick, stainless steel pass, late at night after the dinner rush. Cooler, harsher light than the bar.

### SCENE 1 — The Hook (establishing)
**Visual:** Wide shot from across the street at night, French Quarter block, Calliope's bar glowing warm through the window. Baron passes on the sidewalk, still in his kitchen clothes, not stopping — just passing, the way he does most nights.
**Mood/Lighting:** Cool blue night exterior, warm amber spilling from the bar window.
**Audio:** Distant street ambiance, no dialogue.
**Note:** Hook text ("He says 'I hate it here' — and means the opposite.") is added in post, not requested from Flow.

### SCENE 2A — The Weight: The POS System
**Visual:** Medium close-up, mid-dinner-rush chaos at the host stand/pass. Baron's hands moving fast and sure over a jammed point-of-sale terminal, tickets piling up behind him, blurred staff moving urgently in the background. Focused, not panicked — the guy who fixes it because no one else will.
**Mood/Lighting:** Harsh fluorescent-and-warm mix, motion blur in the background, sharp focus on his hands and face.
**Audio:** Kitchen/dining room chaos, a printer chattering, raised voices in the background (indistinct, not scripted lines).

### SCENE 2B — The Weight: The Website at 1AM
**Visual:** Medium shot, Baron alone at a small prep table after close, still in his apron, laptop open, the rest of the kitchen dark behind him except the glow of the screen and a single work light. Focused, quietly building something that isn't on anyone's schedule but his own.
**Mood/Lighting:** Cool laptop-screen light on his face, warm single bulb overhead, everything else in shadow.
**Audio:** Near silence, faint keyboard clicks, the low hum of an idle kitchen.

### SCENE 2C — The Weight: Carrying It
**Visual:** Wide shot, Baron alone at the pass in the empty, dark kitchen, hands braced on the steel counter, head down for a moment before he straightens up, pulls off his apron, and heads for the door. No one else in frame.
**Mood/Lighting:** Dim, mostly single-source light, long shadows.
**Audio:** Near silence, a heavy exhale, footsteps starting.

### SCENE 3 — He Stops at Her Bar
**Visual:** Medium shot, Baron pushes into Calliope's bar, the warm room settling around him. He drops onto a stool at the counter, shoulders down for the first time all night. Calliope looks up from what she's doing, reads him instantly.
**Mood/Lighting:** Warm amber bar light against the cooler night he just walked in from.
**Audio:** Door sound, bar ambiance, glassware.
**Note:** On-screen text ("On the worst nights — the nights the city leans hardest — he stops at her bar.") is added in post, appearing line by line per the script, not requested from Flow.

### SCENE 4 — "I Hate It Here"
**Visual:** Close-up on Baron's face at the bar, tired but not defeated, a faint almost-smile at the corner of his mouth as he says the line. Calliope in soft focus behind him, listening, unsurprised.
**Dialogue (Baron):** "I hate it here."
**Mood/Lighting:** Warm, low bar light, shallow depth of field on Baron's face.
**Audio:** Room hush, the dialogue line, glass clinking softly in the background.

### SCENE 5 — Calliope Hears It
