## Welcome to the Table

I should have written this one first. It's going up third instead, which feels about right. In a kitchen, nobody waits for the table to be set before the first plates start moving.

So here it is, properly: welcome. This is where the Culinary Crescendo journey gets documented. Two books in so far: *A Symphony of Love in the Big Easy*, and its prequel, *The Scarlet Foundation*. Baron Vieux, Olivia, Bayouboujee, and the New Orleans that made all of them.

Here's what you'll find at this table.

**Writing updates.** Where the next book stands, and the decisions that shape a series while nobody's watching. Just this month, writing the shipping container post raised a question I hadn't answered on the page: how does Baron "own" what he built if the gravel under it is rented? The answer is that he leases before he buys. That's what writing a series actually looks like. Small calls, made one at a time, that have to hold for every book after.

**The New Orleans behind the books.** Real places that shaped fictional ones. Kaldi's was the first, because it's where everything starts. There are more coming, told straight, with a clear line between what happened and what I imagined.

**The people behind the characters.** *The Apothecary Sessions*, the companion podcast, takes one character at a time: the real inspiration, the craft, the lesson underneath. So far: Baron's origin, then Rampart, then Melpomene. Olivia is coming.

**A first look at what's next.** I'll keep this one short, because some things should stay in the pot until they're ready. Olivia's story is stepping forward. More of her on the stage, more of the parts of her the first book only let you glimpse. That's all I'll say for now.

If you came to these books for a love story and found one that's really about ownership, sobriety, and the people who show up for you, you're in the right place. Pull up a chair.

New Orleans keeps its own schedule, and so does this blog. The surest way to catch new posts, chapter previews, and release news is the newsletter.
